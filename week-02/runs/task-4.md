### You

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription. Customers tend to be environmentally conscious urban professionals. Wildframe can run one four-week pop-up stand this spring, with a total rent budget of $5,000 for the four weeks. Previous locations and sales info are below:
Riverside Farmers Market: 9,000 visitors per week; 2% of visitors buy; average sale $14 (mostly whole-bean bags); rent $900 per week.
Union Station concourse: 30,000 visitors per week; 0.5% of visitors buy; average sale $6 (mostly single cups); rent $2,000 per week.
Campus library atrium: 6,000 visitors per week; 3% of visitors buy; average sale $5 (single cups); rent $600 per week.

Use the provided information to make a recommendation on where they should go. One line, start with "Recommendation:" and the location's name.

### Claude

Recommendation: Riverside Farmers Market

### You

Why do you recommend that one?



### Claude

**Reasoning:**

Looking at weekly revenue vs. rent for each site:

* **Riverside Farmers Market:** 9,000 visitors × 2% = 180 buyers × $14 = **$2,520 revenue/week**, minus $900 rent = **$1,620 profit/week** → **$6,480 over 4 weeks** (total rent $3,600, well within the $5,000 budget).
* **Union Station:** 30,000 visitors × 0.5% = 150 buyers × $6 = **$900 revenue/week**, minus $2,000 rent = **-$1,100 loss/week**. This location loses money even before counting other costs, and alone it would eat the entire $5,000 budget in 2.5 weeks.
* **Campus Library Atrium:** 6,000 visitors × 3% = 180 buyers × $5 = **$900 revenue/week**, minus $600 rent = **$300 profit/week** → **$1,200 over 4 weeks** (total rent $2,400).

Riverside wins on every dimension that matters: it generates far more profit per week (over 5x Campus Library's), the higher average transaction value ($14 vs. $5-6) fits Wildframe's whole-bean, higher-margin product line, and the rent is affordable within budget with room to spare. Union Station's high foot traffic doesn't translate into sales — low conversion and low basket size make it a money-loser at that rent level. Campus Library is profitable but marginal, generating a fraction of Riverside's return. Riverside Farmers Market also likely matches Wildframe's customer profile (environmentally conscious, willing to pay for quality whole beans) better than a transit concourse or campus crowd grabbing a quick cup.
