### You

Wildframe Coffee Roasters is a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through a monthly subscription.  Customers tend to be environmentally conscious urban professionals. They want to offer new perks for subscribers who stay 6 months or longer. Give six perk ideas as a numbered list, each one sentence with a max of 25 words.

### Claude

1. Offer a free reusable stainless-steel travel tumbler engraved with their name after six consecutive months of subscription.
2. Provide early access to limited-edition, single-origin micro-lot roasts before they're available to the general public.
3. Give loyal subscribers a discount code for local bike-share or public transit programs, reinforcing their eco-conscious lifestyle.
4. Send a quarterly "sustainability report" showing their personal carbon offset impact from choosing compostable packaging and ethical sourcing.
5. Unlock free priority shipping using carbon-neutral delivery partners exclusively for subscribers past the six-month milestone.
6. Invite long-term subscribers to an annual virtual cupping session with the head roaster to taste rare, sustainably-sourced beans.
