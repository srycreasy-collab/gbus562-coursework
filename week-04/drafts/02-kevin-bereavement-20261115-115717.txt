Per our Bereavement and Family Leave policy, for the death of a grandparent you're entitled to up to 3 paid days off. These days don't need to be taken consecutively, but must be used within 60 days, and this time doesn't reduce your PTO or sick balance. No documentation is required — just let your supervisor know as soon as you can so the time can be arranged. (Source: bereavement-and-family-leave.html)
